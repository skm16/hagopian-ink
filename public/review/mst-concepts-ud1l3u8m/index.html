Newsletter alert bar & pop-up concepts

Three ways to announce “View the new monthly MST Newsletter.” Each mockup below is live. Watch the motion play, try the buttons, and use Replay to see it again.

Prepared by Hagopian Ink
AClassic Center Pop BSpecial Delivery Right Slide CInside This Issue Top Expand
Option A

Classic Center Pop

Top red alert banner with popup emerging in the center blurring the rest of the content until the popup closes. The popup includes an image of your newsletter and a preview of the contents in bullet format.

Option B

Special Delivery Right Slide

Top red alert banner with popup sliding from the right edge of the browser. Popup animates to open an envelope revealing the newsletter design with a simple click to view the newsletter.

Option C

Inside This Issue Top Expand

Top red alert banner with the popup expanding from the top banner below to unfold a highlight of the newsletter contents in 1-2-3-4 steps. The popup adds a slightly darker screen to the content underneath and lifts when the window closes.
